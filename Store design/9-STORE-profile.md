# 9. STORE PROFILE

This is the "Meet the Teacher-Author" block that appears at the bottom of every product
page, and the store page itself. Copy the English text. Everything under INTERNAL NOTES
stays in this file.

## Store name

Magic of Discoveries

## Short line, if the store asks for a tagline

No prep printable resources for the early grades, in English and Spanish.

## About the store

Magic of Discoveries is a small family publisher. My wife Maria and I make printable resources for the early grades, and we draw, write, and lay out every page ourselves.

Our main series is directed drawing: step-by-step pages designed to support independent work, with room to trace the drawing, draw it independently, color it, and then write the word. Every page is black and white, US Letter, and built to print cleanly on a school copier.

Most of what we publish comes in matching English and Spanish editions with the same drawings, so a dual language classroom can use both side by side.

We read every review and appreciate hearing how the resources work in your classroom. If you notice a problem with a resource, please let us know so we can take a look and correct it when needed.

## Location

Miami, Florida, United States

INTERNAL NOTES. DO NOT PASTE INTO TPT.
---------------------------------------------------------------------

On honesty. The text never claims classroom teaching experience, because there is none.
It says plainly who makes the books and how. Teachers on this site can tell the difference
between a colleague and a marketer, and a false claim would surface in the reviews sooner
or later.

On the picture. The block shows a round portrait next to the name, and on every listing
the buyer sees a face there. A real photograph, of one or both of you, builds more trust
than a logo. If a photograph is not wanted, the second best option is a clean square mark
in the same colors as the covers, dark blue and red, so the store and the books look like
one family.
